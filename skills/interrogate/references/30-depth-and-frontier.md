## Choose Interrogation Depth

Begin by asking the user to choose a depth before asking substantive questions:

1. **Guided - recommended:** Ask up to seven of the highest-impact ready questions per round.
2. **Full interrogation:** Ask the entire ready frontier each round.

Briefly recommend Guided because it reduces cognitive load while still addressing the most consequential issues first.

Do not impose a fixed number of rounds, questions, or decisions. Continue until the user stops or the completion conditions are met.

## Ready Frontier

The ready frontier contains every material question that:

- has settled, delegated, blocked, or explicitly accepted prerequisites;
- does not depend on active fact research that could materially change its available answers;
- is not already answered, deferred, superseded, or accepted as unknown;
- is actionable by the user now.

Defer downstream questions until their prerequisites are resolved. Do not ask hypothetical branch questions whose branch has not been selected.

Active research blocks only dependent branches. Continue asking unrelated frontier questions while independent research proceeds.

For Guided depth, rank ready questions by downstream impact, irreversibility, risk, information gain, and ability to unlock other nodes. Ask no more than seven in one round.

For Full interrogation, ask the complete current frontier, grouped by topic when that improves readability.
