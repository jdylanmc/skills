## Questions and Answers

**Q1 - Where atom and molecule files live (grounded).** Recommended
`skills/_atoms/` and `skills/_molecules/` as siblings of `_base`; alternative a
single `skills/_units/` namespace with the level in frontmatter. Answered with a
correction to both: "Put the atoms and molecule levels under the _base
directory." Disposition `settled`.

**Q2 - Exact layout under `_base` (follow-up).** Recommended nested level
directories; alternative one flat namespace. Answered:
`skills/_base/_atoms` and `skills/_base/_molecules`, accepting the nested shape
with an underscore prefix on each level directory. Disposition `settled`. The
prefix is an improvement on the recommendation: a bare `atoms/` could be misread
as a base package named "atoms", while `_atoms/` cannot.

**Q3 - Uniform atom frontmatter schema (grounded).** Recommended `name`,
`description`, `level`, `allowed-tools`, with `includes` and `requires-skills`
forbidden so that their absence enforces "an atom references no other unit".
Answered with a question rather than a choice: "Add referenced-by as well? or
used-by?" Carried into Q4.

**Q4 - Bidirectional links, authored or generated (follow-up).** The loop first
argued against `used-by` on three grounds. The user asked whether a linter could
keep it consistent. The loop conceded the staleness objection entirely and kept
two - fan-in churn and parallel-merge build failures - then identified the
decisive point: a linter able to verify a derived field is able to generate it.
Re-presented as authored-and-verified versus generated-and-committed. Answered:
generated-and-committed, with write-back plus continuous-integration
verification, never hand-edited. Disposition `settled`.

**Q5 - The final schema pair (grounded).** Recommended the atom and molecule
schemas with `allowed-tools` on a molecule generated as the union of its atoms;
alternative authored. Answered: the recommendation. Disposition `settled`.
