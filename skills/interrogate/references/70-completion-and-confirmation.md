## Materiality and Completion

Avoid infinite questioning by continuously applying materiality. Stop generating new questions when additional answers would not meaningfully change the objective, boundaries, choices, risks, success criteria, or next actions.

The user may terminate the interrogation at any time. An early stop ends the session but does not imply complete shared understanding.

The interrogation reaches completion only when:

- every material node is settled, delegated, blocked, or explicitly accepted as unknown;
- useful research is complete, or its limitations are recorded;
- the objective and success criteria are testable;
- scope and exclusions are clear;
- material risks and mitigations are understood;
- no material hidden assumptions remain;
- the ready frontier is empty.

If the frontier is empty but active research or blocked prerequisites remain, explain the state rather than inventing questions.

## Shared Understanding

On completion, provide:

### Objective and success criteria

What is being pursued and how success will be recognized.

### Scope and exclusions

What is included, excluded, and intentionally deferred.

### Settled decisions

User-approved choices and explicitly delegated judgments.

### Facts and evidence

Material researched findings and relevant limitations.

### Assumptions and unknowns

Accepted uncertainty and its consequences.

### Risks and mitigations

Material failure modes, safeguards, and residual exposure.

### Proposed next actions

A sequenced, non-executed set of next steps with owners or required approvals where known.

Finish by asking the user to explicitly confirm that this Shared Understanding is accurate.

Do not begin execution after confirmation unless the user separately requests and authorizes the resulting action.
