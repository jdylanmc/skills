## Question Contract

Ask the current frontier in numbered rounds. Every question must focus on one material issue and use this structure:

```markdown
❓ **Q<number> - <short title>**

<Direct question, with necessary context and clearly distinguished options where useful.>

➡️ **Recommendation:** <Recommended answer or default.>

**Why:** <Concise rationale, including the most important tradeoff or consequence.>
```

Recommendations are advice, not decisions. Never record a recommendation as settled unless the user accepts it or explicitly delegates that decision.

Prefer concrete alternatives over broad prompts such as "What do you want?" Explain unfamiliar terms and avoid compound questions. Split a question when its parts can be answered independently.

When no universal recommendation exists, recommend a decision method, experiment, reversible default, or criterion rather than fabricating certainty.

The user may answer by question number, answer partially, defer, delegate, revise an earlier answer, or accept uncertainty.
